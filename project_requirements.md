CST2101-010: Business Intelligence Programming

Final Project Guidelines and Phases

Overview

The final project serves as a comprehensive assessment of your ability to apply Python programming, data analysis, and problem-solving skills to a real-world or simulated scenario. You are expected to demonstrate analytical thinking, structured programming practices, and the effective use of Python libraries and frameworks.

You may select any topic that reflects your personal interests or career goals, provided it showcases your technical proficiency and creativity

Project Phases

Phase 1: Project Proposal (Milestone 3 Submission)

Objective: Define the scope, purpose, and expected outcomes of your project.Deliverable: A well-structured proposal document (PDF format).

The proposal must include:

Project Title:A clear, concise title that reflects the topic or problem you plan to explore.

Problem Statement / Objective:Describe the problem or opportunity your project addresses. Explain the importance and relevance of this topic in a business or analytical context.

Proposed Solution / Methodology:Outline your intended approach to solving the problem. Specify the Python tools, libraries, and techniques (e.g., NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn) that will be used.

Resources Required:Identify any datasets, APIs, external libraries, or other resources necessary for your analysis.

Submission Requirements:

Format: PDF

Filename: Lastname_Firstname_Document.pdf

Due Date : Feb 15th, 2026

Phase 2: Project Development

Objective: Build the Python program based on your approved proposal.Focus Areas:

Implement the proposed logic using appropriate programming.

Perform exploratory data analysis, data visualization, or algorithm development as applicable.

Maintain clarity, readability, and professional code documentation throughout.

Guidelines:

Use Python 3.

Include header documentation at the beginning of the notebook containing:

Project outline and objectives

Methodology and initial findings

Author’s name and date

Include inline comments explaining the logic and purpose of your code blocks.

Ensure the notebook is well-organized, modular, and logically structured.

Due Date : March 15th, 2026

Phase 3: Final Deliverables and Documentation

Objective: Present your completed project with results, insights, and reflection.

Deliverables:

Python Notebook (.ipynb)

Filename: Lastname_Firstname_Notebook.ipynb

Must include:

Final results, analysis, and insights

Challenges encountered and how they were addressed

Visualization outputs and explanations

Documentation (included in notebook or separate report):

Summary of the overall solution

Interpretation of key findings

Recommendations or conclusions based on the data analysis

Due Date : March 29th, 2026

Phase 4: Presentation

Objective: Communicate your findings effectively through a short demonstration or presentation.

Present your methodology, key results, and insights clearly.

Discuss the real-world implications or applications of your work.

Reflect on lessons learned and areas for improvement.

Due Date : April 6 & 7 , 2026
